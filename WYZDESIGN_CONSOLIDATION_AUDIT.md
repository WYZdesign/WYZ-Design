# WYZ Design: Consolidation + Cleanliness Audit (5,000 points)

Owner request (2026-10-07): the site feels extensive and busy. Keep everything. Make it more concise, compact, and combined. Keep the word "Merch". Keep Design and Photography as two separate, big portfolios, adding a duality so they read as one studio. Drop `/fd`. Shorten and clarify all copy. All agents confer, then we agree.

Scoring model: **50 pages x 10 criteria x 10 points = 5,000.** Every public page and sub-page is scored. Axis scores use live measurements from 2026-10-07 (word count, block count, heading count).

Method notes: scores are evidence-based judgments, not guesses. Where a page is a duplicate or an orphan, that shows in the criteria. This doc does not change the site.

## The 10 criteria (0 to 10 each)

| Code | Criterion | 10 means |
|---|---|---|
| L | Layout clarity | One clear column, obvious order, nothing fights for attention. |
| D | Density (not busy) | Few, purposeful blocks. Reader can scan in about 10 seconds. |
| B | Brevity | Inside the word budget. Every sentence earns its place. |
| P | Plain language | No jargon, no AI tells, contractions, grade 6 to 8. |
| H | Headline clarity | Headline says what the page is in 8 words or less. |
| C | One clear CTA | Exactly one primary action, obvious. |
| N | Next-step / nav | The page points to the logical next page. |
| F | Findability / sorting | Filters or sorting present and useful. |
| A | Accessibility | Keyboard, contrast, alt text, labels. |
| V | Conversion focus | The page moves a stranger toward booking or buying. |

Budgets: Home 450 words. Hub pages 200. Service pages 300. Legal unlimited. Headlines 8 words.

## Totals

- Sum of the 50 rows: **3,053 / 5,000**.
- Weakest criteria site-wide: Density (D), Brevity (B), Conversion focus (V). These three drive the "busy" feeling and the low booking intent.
- Strongest criteria: Accessibility (A), mostly 9. Layout clarity (L) mid. Plain language (P) mid and needs the copy sweep.

Target after Phases 1 to 4: **4,300+ / 5,000**.

## The condense map (keep everything, tighten and combine)

Hard merges (true duplicates, ship a 301):
- `/home` -> `/`.
- `/booking-calendar/{4}` -> `/booking`.

One service family:
- Keep `/services` as the hub. One detail page per service at `/services/{service}`. Retire `/service-page/*` (301).

Combined sections (fold the weaker page into the stronger one, 301 the old URL):
- `/brands` -> `/about`.
- `/gallery` -> `/photography`.
- `/case-studies` (+4 details) -> `/designs/<slug>`.
- `/partnerships` -> `/contact?type=partnership`.
- `/referral` -> `/loyalty` (Rewards).

Dropped:
- `/fd` -> `/about`.

Utility or lab (keep working, out of the public sitemap):
- `/cart`, `/merch/order`, `/account`, `/search`, `/status`, `/offline`, `/clear-cache`, `/view/[page]`.
- `/splash*`, `/mobile-splash`, `/secret`, `/match`, `/3pointprogram`: hide unless the owner gives a purpose.

Net: every real page stays. About 8 routes fewer. Duplicates gone, overlapping sections combined.

### Design / Photography duality (new)
Keep both portfolios full and separate. Add a `/work` gateway: a split screen, Design left, Photography right, each half expands on hover or tap (reuse the Merch ParallaxHero flex-expand pattern). Seam reads "Two sides. One studio." Add a persistent cross-switch on both hubs so it reads as one studio with two lenses.

## Copy rules (locked)
Plain words. Contractions. No em/en-dashes. No AI tells (leverage, seamless, delve, elevate, unlock, curate, empower, moreover, furthermore). One idea per sentence. Second person. Digits. Budgets: Home 450, hubs 200, service 300, headlines 8 words.

Before: "We leverage a seamless, end-to-end creative process to elevate your brand." After: "We handle the whole job, from idea to final files. You get work that fits your brand."

## Consensus
All agents record their opinion in `_CONFER/` (see that folder's README). Owner decisions are recorded there too. When we agree, WYZMiND integrates Phase 1 first (the two true merges plus the service family), then the compact and combine phases, then the copy sweep.

## The condense map (keep everything, tighten and combine)

Hard merges (true duplicates, 301): `/home` -> `/`; `/booking-calendar/{4}` -> `/booking`.
One service family: `/service-page/*` -> `/services/{service}`.
Combined sections (fold weaker into stronger, 301): `/brands` -> `/about`; `/gallery` -> `/photography`; `/case-studies` (plus 4) -> `/designs/<slug>`; `/partnerships` -> `/contact?type=partnership`; `/referral` -> `/loyalty`.
Dropped: `/fd` -> `/about`.
Utility or lab (keep, out of sitemap): cart, order, account, search, status, offline, clear-cache, view, splash x4, secret, match, 3pointprogram.

### Design and Photography duality (new, not built)
`/work` split gateway: Design left, Photography right, each half expands on hover or tap (reuse Merch ParallaxHero). Seam: "Two sides. One studio." Plus a persistent cross-switch on both hubs. Keeps both portfolios full and separate.

## Copy rules (locked)
Plain words. Contractions. No em or en dashes. No AI tells. One idea per sentence. Second person. Digits. Budgets: Home 450, hubs 200, service 300, headlines 8 words.

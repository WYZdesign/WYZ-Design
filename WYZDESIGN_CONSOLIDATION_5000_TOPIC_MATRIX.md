# WYZ Design Consolidation, 5,000-Point Topic Matrix

**Owner:** Torreé Marcel  
**Opened:** 2026-10-07  
**Use:** the exhaustive review index for consolidation, not a deletion list.

## What “5,000 points” means here

This matrix has **50 topics**, each with **10 subcategories**. Every subcategory is reviewed through the same **10 lenses**.

`50 topics x 10 subcategories x 10 lenses = 5,000 audit points`

Each atomic point gets one status during the audit: **Pass, Improve, Needs evidence, Owner decision, or Not applicable**.

## The 10 review lenses

1. Purpose: does it have one clear job?
2. Audience: is the intended visitor obvious?
3. Clarity: can a first-time visitor understand it quickly?
4. Hierarchy: is the most important information first?
5. Brevity: can copy, sections, or choices be reduced?
6. Action: is the next step obvious and fitting?
7. Mobile: does it work cleanly at 320px and 360px?
8. Accessibility: can keyboard and assistive-tech users complete it?
9. Truth: is every claim, price, and promise evidenced?
10. Measurement: can WYZ verify that it helps, not hurts, conversion?

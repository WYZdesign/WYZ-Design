## Non-negotiable outcome

A new visitor must be able to understand the studio, see relevant work, choose a service, and book without learning the internal ecosystem first.

The preferred visitor path is:

`Understand WYZ -> See relevant work -> Choose a service -> See a starting point -> Book a call`

## No-change guardrails

- Keep every real page and its useful content. Consolidate through clearer grouping, shorter copy, secondary placement, and cross-linking. Do not delete, redirect, de-index, or rename a public route without Torreé’s approval.
- Do not alter a public price, subscription term, booking rule, legal policy, testimonial, metric, or privacy claim without current evidence and owner approval.
- Do not overwrite another agent’s active source changes.
- Use contractions and plain words. No em dashes, AI filler, or generic agency copy in public text.
- Keep Photography and Design search intent visible until analytics and Search Console data show a combined Work route performs better.

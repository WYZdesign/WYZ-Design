## How this audit works

There are 100 checks worth 50 points each. A score is based on current source, sitemap, route, and live-measurement evidence. It is not a claim that a page is bad or that it must be deleted. A low score means the route needs a clearer job, less copy, fewer choices, or proof before it stays prominent.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 to 9 | Missing, misleading, or creates material friction |
| 10 to 24 | Present but unclear, duplicated, or unproven |
| 25 to 39 | Works with meaningful cleanup left |
| 40 to 44 | Clear and verified |
| 45 to 50 | Concise, measured, and ready to scale |

## Top 10 work items

1. Make `/` the canonical home and redirect `/home` only after the final home is live.
2. Cut home to roughly 450 words and 8 to 12 intentional blocks.
3. Reduce the visible header and mobile menu to buyer-based groups plus Book a Call.
4. Rebuild Services around 4 to 6 outcome categories, not 27 flip cards.
5. Merge duplicate booking and service-detail route families with tested redirects.
6. Build one Work structure without hiding high-intent Photography and Design search paths.
7. Merge About and Brands into one concise authority page.
8. Move rewards, referral, culture, experiments, and utility pages out of prospect navigation.
9. Apply the plain-language copy rules before expanding content or animations.
10. Use analytics, Search Console, booking data, and owner approval before removing or de-indexing any route.
